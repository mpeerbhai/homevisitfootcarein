Corn & Callus Care at Home

Corns and calluses build up where shoes rub or bones press. Over time they become painful, and for people with diabetes they can hide ulcers underneath. Safe reduction at home brings quick relief.

Call (765) 430-6963

Signs it's time for a visit

  • Hard, thick skin on the bottom of the foot or toes
  • A painful spot that feels like a pebble in the shoe
  • Calluses that crack or bleed

What happens during the home visit

  • Careful, painless reduction of corns and calluses
  • Padding to take pressure off sore spots
  • Footwear advice to keep them from coming back

This page is general information, not medical advice. Please schedule a visit for a diagnosis and a treatment plan that's right for you.
