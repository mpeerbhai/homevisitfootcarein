Who We Serve

House calls are for anyone who finds it hard to get to a foot doctor's office, and for the families and communities who care for them.

Homebound and limited-mobility patients

People who use a walker or wheelchair, are recovering from surgery or a hospital stay, live with dementia or simply can't manage a trip out. We treat them in the comfort of home.

People with diabetes

Diabetes puts feet at risk. Regular foot exams and safe nail and callus care help prevent ulcers and infections, and they're much easier to keep up with when the doctor comes to you.

Families and caregivers

Adult children and caregivers set up most of our visits. You can be there in person or join by phone, and we'll explain what we found and what to watch for.

Assisted living and senior communities

We see residents of assisted living, independent living and memory care communities. Staff can schedule visits for one resident or several on the same day.

Home health and primary care teams

Home health nurses, primary care offices and case managers can refer patients who need a podiatrist but can't travel. We'll keep you informed about the care we provide.

People without transportation

No car, no ride, or a long way from the nearest specialist? In rural parts of our service area, a house call may be the simplest way to get foot care.
