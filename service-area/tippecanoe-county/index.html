Foot care at home in Tippecanoe County

Dr. Peerbhai's West Lafayette Podiatry office serves Tippecanoe County patients who can come in. For those who can't, the doctor comes to them: at home, in apartments and in the many independent living, assisted living and memory care communities around Lafayette and West Lafayette.

Facility staff and family members often call us when a resident has thick or painful nails, a sore that isn't healing or new trouble walking. We can see several residents of the same community in one trip.

Home foot care services in Tippecanoe County

Every service below is available during a house call anywhere in Tippecanoe County.

Diabetic Foot Care at Home

Diabetes can cause nerve damage and poor circulation in the feet, so small cuts, blisters and thick nails turn into serious problems quickly.

Learn more

Toenail Care at Home

Thick, fungal or curved toenails are hard to trim, especially for anyone with poor eyesight, arthritis or trouble bending.

Learn more

Corn & Callus Care at Home

Corns and calluses build up where shoes rub or bones press.

Learn more

Ingrown Toenail Treatment at Home

An ingrown toenail can become infected quickly, especially in older adults and people with diabetes.

Learn more

Foot Wound Care at Home

Foot wounds and diabetic ulcers need regular, careful treatment to heal.

Learn more

Senior Foot Care & Fall Prevention

Painful feet change the way people walk, and that raises the risk of a fall.

Learn more

Dry & Cracked Heel Care

Deep heel cracks can hurt, bleed and let infection in, especially for people with diabetes or poor circulation.

How a house call works

  1. Call us

    Call (765) 430-6963. Tell us who the visit is for, where they live and what's bothering their feet. Family members and caregivers can call too.

  2. We schedule a visit

    We check insurance, find a day the doctor is in your area and confirm the time with you.

  3. The doctor comes to you

    Dr. Peerbhai examines and treats the patient at home or in their senior living community, then sets up follow-up visits if needed.
